Course outcomes
At the end of the course student will be able
CO1: Demonstrate theoretical device/circuit operation in properly constructed analog circuits.
CO2: Able to operate standard test equipment like multi-meters, oscilloscopes, power supplies,
waveform generators, and to analyze, test, and implement circuits in breadboard.
CO3: Able to analyze the operation of an active device and compare its performance with the expected
performance given in the data sheets.
CO4: Able to apply troubleshooting techniques to test the circuits.
CO5: Able to analyze the circuits using the simulation tools.
