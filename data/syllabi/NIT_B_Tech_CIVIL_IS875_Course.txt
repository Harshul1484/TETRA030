Course outcomes:
On completion of the course, the students will be able to:
calculate shape factor and plastic moment capacity
design eccentrically loaded compression members (Beam-Columns) and their base plates
design welded plate girder and other components and Gantry girder
carry out wind load calculations for tall structures and design of steel chimneys
design light gauge steel sections
