CE211 BUILDING PLANNING AND DRAWING
Course objectives:
To understand the principles of planning and bylaws
To draw plan, elevation and section of load bearing and framed structures
To draw plan, elevation and section of public and industrial structures
To prepare detailed working drawing for doors, windows, etc.
Course Content
Classification of buildings - Principles of planning - Dimensions of buildings - Building bye-laws for floor
area ratio, open spaces - Orientation of buildings - Lighting and Ventilation- Planning and preparing
sketches and working drawings of Residential buildings (Flat and sloping roof), Schools, Hostels,
Hospitals, Single-storey factory buildings with trusses. Detailed working drawings of the component
parts - Doors and Windows - Roof Trusses - Staircases-Toilets
